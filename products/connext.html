How it works

Connext® Surgical Matrix

01

Fills the dead space

Obliterates the cavity where seroma, hematoma and infection begin.

02

Absorbs the fluid

Soaks up exudate so seroma and hematoma have nowhere to collect.

03

Protective gel barrier

Sets into a moisture barrier with a low-pH antimicrobial environment.

04

Resorbs

Nothing to remove. It dissolves as the site heals.
