How to use it

Open it any time: press Ctrl + Alt + T anywhere on your PC, or double-click Talk to Text on your desktop. It starts listening on its own.

Put your words anywhere: talk, then click where you want the words (an email, Word, a website) and press Ctrl + V. With "Copy as I speak" on, everything you said is already copied.

Keys in this window: Ctrl+Space start or stop. Ctrl+Enter copy all. Esc stop.

Every sentence starts with a capital on its own. Nothing is lost: your words are saved as you go, and New keeps a copy in History.

If it says it can't hear you: check the microphone in the Windows sound settings, or click the mic icon at the left of Chrome's address bar and choose Allow.

Words it gets wrong

One per line: what it hears, an equals sign, what you want written. It fixes these every time.
