The stations

Three systems. One line.

Lotus Medical carries full-line lower extremity fixation, toe to tibia. Each station walks the procedure start to finish, instrument in hand.

STATION 01Forefoot

MiniLock™

Minimally invasive bunion plating. Shift the capital fragment, lock it and compress across the osteotomy through a 1–2 cm incision.

STATION 02Midfoot

LapiLock™

4D Lapidus correction. Frontal, transverse and sagittal correction from one jig, before a single cut. 13+ fixation options.

STATION 03Ankle

FibFix™

The low-profile intramedullary fibula nail. Rigid at both ends, flexible in the middle. Minimal soft-tissue disruption.
