MiniLock · Minimally Invasive Bunion Plating
Minimally invasive bunion system

Bunion correction, locked in.

MiniLock is a small titanium plate that seats inside the canal through a 1–2 cm medial incision, then locks the capital fragment where the surgeon puts it.

Watch it work

Shift it. Lock it. Keep it.

What is happening
A transverse osteotomy.
Stability

Shifts fail three ways. MiniLock resists all three.

ROTATION

Locked capital fragment

Two 2.4 mm distal locking screws lock the head to the plate.

PISTONING

Compression across the cut

A 2.5 mm lag screw compresses the osteotomy.

DRIFT

Proximal anchor

A 2.5 mm proximal screw anchors the plate in the shaft, reducing torsion and pistoning.

IN THE CANAL

Low profile by design

The plate sits inside the canal, not on the medial wall.

Guided placement

Guided, not freehand.

Left and right versions. Screws FDA cleared under K211843.

Surgical technique

Ten steps. One small incision.

01
  1. Set up the construct
  2. Incision and release
  3. Transverse osteotomy
  4. Open the canal
  5. Seat the construct
  6. Wire and derotate
  7. Drill and read length
  8. Lock the capital fragment
  9. Compress the osteotomy
  10. Anchor proximally and close

Refer to the MiniLock surgical technique guide for complete instructions.
