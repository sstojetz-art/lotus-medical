The evidence · fibular nails as a class

Nail or plate? The trials answer.

0.23
Relative risk of infection, nail vs. plate

Seven randomized trials, 586 patients. Infection risk with the nail was about a quarter of plating (p < 0.0001). Function equal at 3, 6 and 12 months; union no different.

The honest partThe same analysis found more hardware failure with the nail (RR 2.05), most in older patients. FibFix is built around that problem: diameters to 5.7 mm, threaded proximal purchase and four distal locks.
