FibFix · Fibula Nail
Low-profile intramedullary fibula nail

Fix the fibula from the inside.

FibFix stabilizes fibula fractures and osteotomies from a minimally invasive entry at the fibula tip. No lateral plate. Minimal soft-tissue disruption.

Surgical technique

Ten steps. Minimally invasive.

01
  1. Template and size
  2. Reduce the fracture
  3. Place the guide wire
  4. Open the canal
  5. Ream and read length
  6. Insert the nail
  7. Attach the targeting arm
  8. Lock distally
  9. Syndesmosis fixation
  10. Place the end cap

Refer to the FibFix surgical technique guide for complete instructions.
