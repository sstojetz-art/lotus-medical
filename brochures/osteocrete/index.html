The magnesium difference

Built from what bone already uses.

0–0%of the body’s magnesium is stored in bone, its main reservoir (NIH).

Magnesium shapes hydroxyapatite crystal formation, promotes osteoblast adhesion and proliferation, and helps determine bone strength. Its deficiency is tied to poor bone quality and osteoporosis.
