Peroneal tendon repair · Surgical technique

Repair. Wrap. Close.

  1. Expose and assess. Incision along the retromalleolar groove; isolate peroneus brevis, protect the sural nerve, debride the tear.
  2. Repair. Primary repair with suture of choice; tubularize the tendon.
  3. Hydrate. BMAC can be used to hydrate the implant.
  4. Wrap. Wrap Integrity around the tendon over the affected area.
  5. Secure and close. Running 4-0 absorbable suture, then repair sheath and retinaculum.

Post-op: 3 weeks non-weight bearing, then 3 weeks protected weight bearing in a boot; PT at week 3 to 4. Anika Integrity Peroneal Tendon Repair Surgical Technique Guide, AML-900-690.
