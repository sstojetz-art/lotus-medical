Step 4B · Mold Mg OsteoCrete into Mg OsteoRod

Close it. Cure it. Seat it.

  1. Shape and place. Form the putty to roughly the rod length and lay it in the base, plug already seated.
  2. Close the mold. Align the posts on the top with the holes in the base.
  3. Lock and cure. Thread the wing nuts on the underside. Cure for 30 minutes. Trim any extruded excess now or after curing.
  4. Release. Remove the wing nuts and the top, then lift the rod out with care. Ready for the canal.
Operative technique

Four steps. A familiar workflow.

1

Set up

Position as for intramedullary nailing of the target bone. For periprosthetic infection, follow the preferred approach to the joint.

Clear orthogonal fluoroscopic views are essential.

2

Approach

Standard exposure to the preferred nail start point. Open the canal with any opening reamer or drill 13mm or greater.

With prior IM implants, use the accepted incisions for hardware removal.

3

Remove & debride

Total or subtotal implant removal when applicable, then debridement per accepted practice and surgeon preference.

4

Insert

Seat the molded Mg OSTEOROD in the canal under fluoroscopic guidance to the preferred or maximum depth.
