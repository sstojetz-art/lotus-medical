How it works

Fixation and biology in one implant.

Day 1 · Fix

Drives like a screw

Threaded human cortical bone, placed over a K-wire with standard drill and tap steps. Compression across the non-union, then trimmed flush. No head.

Weeks · Integrate

A scaffold, not a foreign body

Human cortical bone is an osteoconductive scaffold. Host bone grows onto and into it while the screw holds the reduction.

Months · Remodel

Becomes the patient's bone

Creeping substitution replaces the graft with living bone. Several months after surgery the screw is no longer distinguishable from host bone.

Remodeling: Brcic et al., cited in J Orthop Traumatol 2023. Technique: Surgebright Shark Screw® Hand & Wrist Surgical Technique.
